Section 1: Introduction

Apex Legends is a team-based battle royale game. The Apex Legends Global Series (ALGS) is sponsored by Electronic Arts Inc. (EA) and governed by its Official Rules (Section 1).

This rulebook summarizes the Apex Legends Global Series Year 4 Official Rules (v7), published by EA in December 2024. These Official Rules may be updated by EA without notice as necessary (Section 2.4). Where this summary and the official document differ, the official document governs.

Section 2: Equipment

Competitors may register and compete on a Supported Platform: a single PSN account on PlayStation 4 or PlayStation 5, a single Xbox account on Xbox One or Xbox Series X|S, or a single EA account on PC (Section 4.1).

  • Live Events: any Live Events are available on the PC platform only (Section 4.1).
  • Pro League Regular Season: players must have a compatible PC to support and play the Game (Section 2.1).
  • Accounts: competitors need a valid EA account and a valid Battlefy account (Section 2.1).
  • Input device: competitors may use a keyboard and mouse or a PlayStation or Xbox controller, and must use the same Input Device for the entirety of each match (Section 5.1).
  • Controllers: competitors can bring their own controllers, which must first be reviewed and approved by Event Officials (Section 4.3).
  • Communication devices: competitors may not possess personal communication devices while actively engaged in a match (Section 4.3).

Section 3: Playing Area

ALGS events are divided by Region, and eligible countries and territories for each Region are listed in the Official Rules (Section 3.1, Appendix B).

  • Lobby size: in the final round of the Playoffs, Championship, Last Chance Qualifier and Pro League Qualifier, twenty (20) Teams compete (Appendix C3).
  • Groups: Teams are seeded into groups of ten (10) at the start of each Regular Season Split (Section 3.3.1).
  • Online tournaments: all Competitors are required to physically participate from an eligible country or territory for the Region for which the Team is registered (Section 2.3.3).
  • Live Events: events may require in-person participation at a specific place and time at a live venue (Section 1).

Section 4: Players & Officials

  • Team size: Competitors join or create Teams of at least three (3) but no more than four (4) Competitors. EA retains a right of approval over the roster submitted by each Team, and may reject any roster that does not comply with the Official Rules (Section 2.3.1).
  • Registration: Teams are required to register for each ALGS event separately (Section 2.2.1). Competitors and Coaches may not be a member of more than one registered Team, with one stated exception for Pro League Roster Competitors in Challenger Circuit competitions (Section 2.3.1).
  • Accounts: each Competitor must have a valid Battlefy account (Section 2.1).
  • Coaches: each Team may identify up to one (1) non-playing individual authorized to communicate with players in official ALGS communication channels between matches. The Coach is not considered a Competitor (Section 2.3.1).
  • Team Captain: the Competitor who creates the Team on the Tournament Site is deemed the Team Captain, who manages the official roster (Section 2.3.2).
  • Officials: Competitors agree to be bound by the Official Rules and the decisions of EA, ALGS officials and ALGS administrators (Section 2.4). EA's decisions are final and binding on all matters related to the ALGS (Section 9.3.1).

Section 5: Rules of Play

Each ALGS round is a series of matches in which Teams accumulate points (Appendix C5.1).

  • Event Officials: Competitors must follow the instructions of Event Officials at all times (Appendix A, A1).
  • Stage format: the Playoffs and Championship use the Group Stage and Double Elimination Bracket Stage format (Section 3.6.2, Section 3.8.2).
  • Match Point format: the Regional Finals, Playoffs, Championship, Last Chance Qualifier and Pro League Qualifier use the Match Point mechanic in the final round. Once a Team reaches fifty (50) points it is "Match Point Eligible" (Appendix C4).
  • Winning under Match Point: once a Team is Match Point Eligible, winning a match (placing first) after the match in which it reached the threshold results in that Team being declared the winner of the event (Appendix C4).
  • Streaming: Competitors that choose to stream their matches must use a minimum ten (10) minute delay (Section 5.1).
  • Soft play: a Competitor not giving their best effort or not taking reasonable and fair actions to gain advantages against opponents in a match is treated as collusion (Appendix A, A2).

Section 6: Scoring

At the end of each match, Teams are awarded points for their placement and the Team's total number of kills (the "Match Score") (Appendix C5.1). Placement points are:

  • 1st place: 12 points (Appendix C5.1(A))
  • 2nd place: 9 points (Appendix C5.1(A))
  • 3rd place: 7 points (Appendix C5.1(A))
  • 4th place: 5 points (Appendix C5.1(A))
  • 5th place: 4 points (Appendix C5.1(A))
  • 6th–7th place: 3 points each (Appendix C5.1(A))
  • 8th–10th place: 2 points each (Appendix C5.1(A))
  • 11th–15th place: 1 point each (Appendix C5.1(A))
  • 16th–20th place: 0 points (Appendix C5.1(A))

Each kill earns 1 point (Appendix C5.1(B)). A Team's Round Score includes its Match Scores for all matches in that round or match series (Appendix C5.1).

If a Team misses a match, with no Competitors from the Team playing in it, the Team receives a Match Score of zero (0) for that match (Appendix C5.1).

Ties in total Placement Points are resolved by the methods described in Appendix C6.2 (Section 3.3.3).

Section 7: Violations & Penalties

Violation of the Official Rules or Event Rules may, at EA's discretion, result in penalties, disqualification, change in match results, loss of winner status, and/or forfeiture of prizes (Appendix A, A6).

  • Roster violations: EA may reject any roster that does not comply with the Official Rules (Section 2.3.1).
  • Cheating: any Competitor may be disqualified for using any cheats, hacks or other third-party "helper" applications in playing Game matches (Section 7).
  • Exploits: taking advantage of known exploits in the Game is prohibited, and it is the responsibility of players to understand and avoid all current illegal exploits (Section 7, Appendix A, A10).
  • Collusion: any action or agreement to disadvantage other Competitors, to predetermine the outcome of a match, or to limit efforts to win a match is strictly prohibited (Appendix A, A2).
  • Unsportsmanlike conduct: abusive or disorderly behavior, including any use of harassing, negative or profane language online, is prohibited (Section 7).
  • Penalties: penalties may include a warning, reprimand, forfeiture of single or all matches, reduction in Match Score, loss of points, temporary or permanent suspension, and disqualification (Appendix A, A6).
  • Team disqualification: disqualification of a Competitor may result in the disqualification of the Competitor's entire Team from an ALGS event or the entire ALGS (Section 7).
  • Rule changes: the Official Rules may be updated by EA without notice as necessary (Section 2.4).

Section 8: Safety Considerations

The Official Rules address safety-related matters through age requirements and substance rules.

  • Minimum age: players from South Korea must be at least eighteen (18), players from Japan at least seventeen (17), and all other players at least sixteen (16) at the time of registration (Section 2.1).
  • Parental consent: for players under the age of majority in their territory of residence, a parent or legal guardian must review and consent to the Official Rules on their behalf and accompany them to any in-person Live Events (Section 2.1).
  • Drugs and alcohol: Competitors may not be under the influence of drugs or alcohol while participating in the ALGS, and use of alcohol or drugs is prohibited on any Live Event premises (Appendix A, A3).
  • Smoking: smoking, including e-cigarettes and vaporizers, is prohibited during any Live Event except in designated areas (Appendix A, A3).
