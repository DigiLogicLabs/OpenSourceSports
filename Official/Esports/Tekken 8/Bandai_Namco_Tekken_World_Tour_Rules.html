Section 1: Introduction

Tekken 8 is a 3D fighting game published by Bandai Namco Entertainment (BNE). Its sanctioned competitive circuit is the Tekken World Tour (TWT). This rulebook summarizes the Official Rules, Tekken World Tour 2026, which BNE publishes on its website. Always check the official page for the current text.

Section references below (Section 3, Section 4, Section 6, Section 10) are the section numbers used in that document.

TWT events come in tiers: Master+, Master, Challenger and Dojo (Section 3). The tier decides some format details, such as which matches are best-of-five (Section 4).

Section 2: Equipment

  • Platform: Matches must be played on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S or Steam (Section 4).
  • Controller: Programmable or turbo controllers are prohibited. This covers macros, combination inputs not built into the game, and single inputs that combine movement and attack. Organizers may inspect controllers (Section 4).

Section 3: Playing Area

Tekken 8 has no physical playing area. Matches are played in Versus mode, using the in-game settings required by Section 4:

  • Default tournament settings (Section 4)
  • Rounds: 3 out of 5 (Section 4)
  • Time limit: 60 seconds per round (Section 4)
  • Stage: random (Section 4)
  • Special Style: allowed (Section 4)
  • Character Customizations: off (Section 4)

Section 4: Players & Officials

  • Eligibility: Section 6 (Eligibility) requires players to be of the age of majority or to have parental permission.
  • Coaches: Unless an organizer sets its own rules, a player may use one coach, and only if they lost the previous Set (Section 4).
  • Conduct: Section 10 (Code of Conduct) requires good sportsmanship and honesty, and prohibits cheating, collusion, hacking, impersonation and harassment.

Section 5: Rules of Play

Bracket

Events use a double-elimination bracket. The Winner's Bracket champion meets the Loser's Bracket champion in Grand Finals (Section 4).

Set length (Section 4)

  • Master+ and Master Events: Matches are best-of-three. Top 8 Winner's Semi-Finals, Losers Round 1, Losers Quarter-Finals, Losers Semi-Finals, Winner's Finals and Grand Finals are best-of-five (Section 4).
  • Challenger and Dojo Events: Matches are best-of-three. Winner's Finals, Losers Semi-Finals, Losers Finals and Grand Finals are best-of-five. Organizers may choose a best-of-five Top 8 format, and must decide before the event starts (Section 4).
  • Global Finals: Top 16 matches are best-of-three and Top 8 matches are best-of-five (Section 4).
  • Grand Finals: If the Loser's Bracket player wins the first set of the series, a second match is played, and players may change characters (Section 4).

Character and stage selection

  • The winner of a Set keeps their character until they lose a Set (Section 4).
  • The loser of each Set chooses the next stage or character (Section 4).
  • Players may request a blind pick (Section 4).

Costumes and characters

  • Preset costumes are legal unless BNE bans specific ones (Section 4).
  • Players cannot use the same preset. A rock-paper-scissors tiebreaker decides conflicts (Section 4).
  • DLC characters may be used starting on their official release date, based on local time zone. Early-access periods are not allowed (Section 4).

Attendance

Players must be ready within 10 minutes of the call time or risk forfeiting the match (Section 4).

Coaching

Consultation is limited to one time per match and one minute. Phones and other devices are prohibited (Section 4).

Section 6: Scoring

  • Round: With the Versus setting "3 out of 5" rounds, the first player to win three rounds wins the game. Each round has a 60-second time limit (Section 4).
  • Set: A set is won by winning the required number of games: two of three in a best-of-three, or three of five in a best-of-five (Section 4).
  • Draws: A tied Set (double KO or timeout) is not scored and is replayed with the same characters (Section 4).

Section 7: Violations & Penalties

  • Pausing or disconnecting: A player who pauses the game, intentionally or accidentally, including through a controller disconnect, forfeits the current round (Section 4).
  • Disruptions outside the players' control: These lead to a restart at the prior round count (Section 4).
  • Disruption during an animation: A disruption during an unfinished animation after unpausing does not cause a forfeit (Section 4).
  • Coaching during a live set: Coaching during a live Set is not allowed (Section 4).
  • Prohibited controller functions: Programmable or turbo controllers are prohibited, and organizers may inspect controllers (Section 4).
  • Illegal preset or customization: Using one can cause a Set to go unscored (Section 4).
  • No-show: A player not ready within 10 minutes of the call time risks forfeiting the match (Section 4).
  • Misconduct: Violations of the Code of Conduct can lead to disqualification, future bans and loss of prizes (Section 10).

Section 8: Safety Considerations

The related requirements in the official rules are:

  • Age and consent: Section 6 (Eligibility) requires players to be of the age of majority or to have parental permission.
  • Conduct: Section 10 (Code of Conduct) prohibits harassment, cheating and similar behavior.
  • Equipment integrity: Organizers may inspect controllers for prohibited functions (Section 4).
