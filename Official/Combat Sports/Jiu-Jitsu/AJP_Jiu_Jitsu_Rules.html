Section 1: Introduction

The Abu Dhabi Jiu-Jitsu Pro (AJP) is the premier professional jiu-jitsu circuit operated under the auspices of the Abu Dhabi Jiu-Jitsu Federation (AJP). This rulebook supersedes all previous editions and serves as the definitive guide for competition standards across the AJP Tour, including World Pro Championship events. Article 1.1 establishes that all matches must be contested according to these regulations unless a specific tournament directive overrides them for special circumstances.

AJP ruleset is designed to maximize athlete safety while maintaining high-intensity grappling. It incorporates specific modifications to standard International Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Federation (IBJJF) rules, particularly regarding submission legality and scoring thresholds for higher belt ranks. Article 1.2 mandates that all competitors adhere to the belt classification system recognized by both AJP and UAEJJF.

Section 2: Equipment

All competitors must wear approved equipment as defined in Article 3.0. The following items are mandatory for Gi competition:

  • mat: The playing surface must be AJP-approved tatami or equivalent, meeting thickness and fire-retardancy standards.
  • gi: Competitors must wear a white, blue, or black Gi approved by the AJP Technical Committee. The fabric weight must not exceed 280g/m² for adult males and 195g/m² for adult females, per Article 3.2.
  • belt: The belt must match the color of the Gi and indicate rank (White, Blue, Purple, Brown, Black). Belts must be sewn securely to the Gi pants and top. Article 3.5.

For No-Gi competition, competitors must wear a rashguard and shorts/grappling pants. Article 3.6. Mouthguards are mandatory for all competitors in No-Gi events and recommended for Gi events. Article 3.7. Protective cups are permitted but not mandatory for male competitors in No-Gi events.

Section 3: Playing Area

The match area is defined by a square mat with dimensions of 8m x 8m, as specified in Article 4.1. The mat must be divided into a center match area of 5m x 5m and an outer safety zone. The mat surface must be free of debris, liquids, or objects that could cause injury.

  • Mat thickness must be at least 4 cm for all events, per Article 4.2.
  • The mat must be constructed of tatami-style material or equivalent approved by the AJP Technical Committee.
  • For Grand Slam events, multiple mats may be used to accommodate simultaneous matches. Article 4.3.

Section 4: Players & Officials

All matches are contested head-to-head between two competitors. Article 5.1. The match duration is determined by the competitor's belt rank, as outlined in Article 5.2:

  • White Belt: 4 minutes
  • Blue Belt: 5 minutes
  • Purple Belt: 6 minutes
  • Brown Belt: 7 minutes
  • Black Belt: 8 minutes (10 minutes for World Pro Championship)

Weight classes are divided into Adult Male and Female categories, with specific kg limits defined in Article 5.3. Age categories include Juvenile (under 18), Adult (18-40), and Masters (40+). Article 5.4.

A match is officiated by a panel consisting of one mat referee, one scoring official, and one timekeeper. Article 5.5. The referee is responsible for the safety of the competitors and enforcing rules, while the scoring official records points and penalties. Article 5.6.

Section 5: Rules of Play

Competitors must maintain a grip on the Gi or No-Gi uniform. Article 6.1. Competitors must remain in constant contact with their opponent, except when executing a takedown or sweep. Article 6.2.

AJP Scoring

  • 2 points: Takedown, Sweep, Knee-on-Belly (Guard Pass). Article 6.3.
  • 3 points: Guard Pass (from back to side). Article 6.4.
  • 4 points: Mount, Back Mount. Article 6.5.

Submissions are awarded when a competitor is forced to tap out or verbally submits. Article 6.6. Joint locks and chokes are legal, subject to belt restrictions (e.g., no heel hooks for White/Blue belts). Article 6.7.

Penalties + Advantages

  • Stalling, fleeing match, illegal grip → penalties. Article 6.8.
  • 4 penalties = Disqualification (DQ). Article 6.9.
  • Advantage awarded for near-completion of scoring moves; tiebreaker only. Article 6.10.

Section 6: Scoring

The decision priority for match outcomes is as follows: submission > points > advantages > penalties > referee decision. Article 7.1. Points are awarded for controlling the opponent's body or limbs, as detailed in Article 7.2.

  • AJP Tour points: each Grand Slam round contributes ranking points; year-end championship tour standings.
  • World Pro Championship: highest annual purse in BJJ (~$50K+ for gold).
  • Gold/Silver/Bronze per weight + belt + age.

Referee decisions are made based on the cumulative points and advantages at the end of the match. Article 7.3. If a competitor is unable to continue due to injury, the match ends immediately. Article 7.4.

Section 7: Violations & Penalties

  • Stalling: verbal warning → penalty; repeated = DQ. Article 8.1.
  • Fleeing: exiting match area without tactical reason = penalty. Article 8.2.
  • Illegal submissions for belt/age: immediate DQ if applied. Article 8.3.
  • Slamming: DQ. Article 8.4.
  • Gi-grip violations: grabbing inside cuffs/pants → penalty. Article 8.5.
  • Unsportsmanlike conduct: DQ. Article 8.6.
  • Anti-doping: AJP anti-doping program; in-event + out-of-competition testing. Article 8.7.

Section 8: Safety Considerations

AJP places a high priority on athlete safety. Article 9.1. The referee has the authority to stop a match at any time for safety reasons, including but not limited to severe injury or exhaustion. Article 9.2.

  • Same injury profile as BJJ generally: joint injuries (shoulder, elbow, knee), occasional concussion.
  • AJP belt-graded submission restrictions enforced; high-risk submissions (heel hooks, knee reaps, twisters) only legal at higher belt levels in specific no-gi categories.
  • Slamming prohibited. Article 9.3.

On-site EMT + medical doctor at Grand Slam and World Pro level; AED on-site. Article 9.4. Skin-infection screening before mat use. Concussion protocol per AJP medical guidelines. Pre-match medical check. Article 9.5.
